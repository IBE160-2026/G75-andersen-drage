G75-andersen-drage — UX Design Validation Report

.docs/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-G75-andersen-drage-2026-11-10/ (DESIGN.md + EXPERIENCE.md)

Adequate. Downstream consumers can use the pair for the core study loop. UJ-1..UJ-3 are fully walked, every {token} reference resolves, all 23 colors come in light/dark pairs, contrast is computed for the main pairs, and DESIGN.md follows the canonical section order. The pair is not yet a clean contract, for four reasons. Stale [OPEN] markers and English PRD strings contradict the Microcopy table. The FR-12 change to a PRD load-bearing differentiator has not been settled with the PRD. The Demo Mode model (picker vs. global mode) is undefined. Innstillinger has no components at all. Fix the 4 high rubric findings before bmad-architecture and story creation consume the pair.

The two extra reviewers make the picture worse than "adequate" suggests, and each raises 2 criticals. The accessibility reviewer finds the pair not AA-ready: the UJ-1 climax, the Quote highlight in the source panel, is about 1.04:1 against the active Passage in Lesesal, and the 'Dine notater' Note row nests buttons inside a clickable row, which fails the NFR-6 axe gate by construction. Focus management (after marking, archiving, answering and reference jumps), heading/landmark structure, shortcut scoping and 320px reflow are also unspecified. The examiner reviewer predicts a B for criterion 4, with an A possible and a C likely if Should features ship half-styled. The design trail is invisible as delivered (dot-folders, Excalidraw JSON, no images, template README), and the remaining scope does not fit about 33 solo days to 2026-12-15 without a firm Must/Should cut. Several rubric findings recur across reviewers (stale strings, the PRD gap, the mobile top bar, the reference-jump return path, archive feedback, wireframe drift). After deduplication: 4 critical · 16 high · 28 medium · 22 low.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Not AA-ready. Fix before status final. The text contrast table holds up: every ratio I recomputed matches within rounding. The spines then use that table to imply AA conformance, and it does not cover what will actually fail. The product's climax, the Quote highlight, is close to invisible in Lesesal. The 'Dine notater' row nests two buttons inside a clickable row. Global single-key shortcuts have no off switch and no focus scoping. Focus after mark-and-advance, archive, quiz answer and the desktop reference jump is either unspecified or wrong. No surface defines headings, landmarks or page titles. Reflow is only promised at 360px, but WCAG 1.4.10 requires 320px.

Unlisted pairs that fail: highlight vs passage-active 1.04 / 1.89; highlight-ring vs passage-active 1.32 (light); passage-active vs surface-source 1.09 / 1.11; kbd-hint inside a primary button 1.30 / 1.35; error-bg vs mastery-shaky-bg 1.01 / 1.10. Counts: critical 2 · high 7 · medium 12 · low 8.

critical

The Quote highlight is effectively invisible against the active Passage

DESIGN Colors / Quote highlight; EXPERIENCE Reference jump; SC 1.4.11, 1.4.1, 1.3.1
#F3E3A3 on #EFE6D2 is 1.04:1 and the 1px ring 1.32:1. This is the UJ-1 climax ("highlights the exact sentence"). A student with low vision, or anyone on a washed-out laptop panel, sees one beige block. Screen readers do not announce <mark> by default, so the highlight conveys nothing non-visually.
Fix: Stop stacking the highlight on passage-active: drop the Passage tint while a Quote is highlighted, or use a ≥3:1 ring (e.g. 2px accent underline/outline). Bracket the Quote with visually hidden "Sitat start" / "Sitat slutt", or reference it via aria-describedby. Add the pair to the contrast table.
critical

The Note row nests interactive controls

DESIGN Note row; EXPERIENCE Note row, Interaction Primitives; SC 4.1.2, 1.3.1
"Whole row is the hit area" plus two separate Tab stops inside it is invalid nesting (axe nested-interactive, serious). It fails the NFR-6 gate by construction, and screen readers flatten or swallow the inner controls. The Arkiv row inherits it, and nothing says what clicking an archived row does.
Fix: Title as a real <a> with a ::after stretched hit area; badge and 'Arkiver' as DOM siblings raised with position: relative; z-index; <ul>/<li> markup. State that an Arkiv row opens nothing.
high

Global single-key shortcuts cannot be turned off and are not scoped to focus

EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives › Keyboard, Flashcard, Quiz Question; SC 2.1.4, 2.1.1
Suppressed only in text fields. Speech-input users trigger them by dictating words. With focus on "Kilde: P4", 'Forklar enklere' or 'Kunne det', a single Space both activates the control and flips or marks the card. Nothing says they are suppressed in dialogs, or what Space does on a revealed card.
Fix: Fire only when focus is in the Flashcard region and not on a button/link/checkbox; add an Innstillinger toggle "Hurtigtaster"; suppress in dialogs; Space on a revealed card is a no-op; use 1–4 (not A–D) for quiz options under the same scoping, or arrow keys in a radio group.
high

Examiner review (grading guide, criterion 4)

review-grading-guide.md

The guide is clear about the order of work: step 4, "Vurder design", happens in the running app; the planning documents are not read until step 6. The A–B descriptor is earned or lost on the built screens. The spines can only earn "spor av designarbeid" directly; everything else is a promise I will check against the app. Right now the repo has no screenshots of anything, and the only visual artifacts sit in a dot-folder inside a dot-folder.

Overall prediction: a B is realistic and an A is possible, only if the Must screens ship polished in one mode with working error/empty states, and the repo shows rendered wireframes plus final screenshots linked from the README.
