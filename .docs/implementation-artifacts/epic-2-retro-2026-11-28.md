No test. **Defer** to Story 3.5 (interrupted Rounds), which owns the Round lifecycle.
- **F3 A flashcard-only Round completes with `push`, not `replace` (low).** `Round.tsx:174` vs the quiz path `Round.tsx:214`; Back from Notatoversikt lands on the finished Round URL (`note.missing`). Unreachable with the samples; the e2e at `round.spec.ts:842-858` uses `goto`, not `goBack`. **Fix** together with F5 (one-line change plus a `goBack` assertion).
